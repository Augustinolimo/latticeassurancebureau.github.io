HOW IT WORKS

Sign once. Verify anywhere.

No special app is needed to check a document. Anyone with a phone can do it.

You issue

Your institution signs a document, a record or a whole batch at once.

You attach a QR slip

Each item gets a QR verification slip carrying your institution's name, printed or digital.

Anyone verifies

A buyer, employer or bank scans the QR and sees whether the record is authentic and unchanged.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Does this prove a physical item, like a painting or a bag, is real?

It proves that the issuing institution signed a record about the item and that the record hasn't been altered. Linking that record to the physical item depends on a tamper-evident tag or label applied by the issuer. We'll advise on this when you apply.

Can a forger copy the QR code?

A QR code can be copied, but a signed record can't be forged. Verification checks the signature and the issuer's name, so a fake with a copied QR will not match the item it's attached to.

Do we need to buy or hold any cryptocurrency?

No. Institutions don't buy or hold any token. We're planning a simple monthly subscription and will share details when your application is approved.

Is LAB Vault audited or certified?

Not yet. It's an early pilot and hasn't been independently audited. We say so openly and onboard institutions carefully.

Who is accepted?

Only institutions that pass our checks. We review every application before access is given, because your integrity protects everyone who relies on the verification.
