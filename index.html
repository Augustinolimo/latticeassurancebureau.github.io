03 / FLAGSHIP DEVELOPMENT
PROTOTYPE STAGE

Introducing
LAB Vault.

A file-security prototype exploring hybrid key establishment, authenticated encryption, and digital signatures.

LAB Vault is being developed as a practical learning and research project. We are testing its behaviour and reviewing its implementation as we work toward a clearer understanding of its security properties.

  • ✓ Authenticated file-encryption workflow
  • ✓ Exploration of hybrid key establishment
  • ✓ Digital-signature verification concepts
Responsible disclosure

LAB Vault is a prototype and has not been independently security-audited. It is not represented as certified or production-ready. Do not use it to protect real customer or sensitive data at this stage.
